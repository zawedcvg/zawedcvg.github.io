Seemingly Plausible Distractors

Using a novel benchmark of carefully constructed trick questions, this paper exposes a troubling blind spot in large language models: when presented with false-but-plausible reasoning paths, they reliably follow the wrong trail. The condition appears widespread across both open and proprietary models, worsens as distractors grow more sophisticated.
